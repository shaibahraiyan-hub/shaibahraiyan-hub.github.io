My Story

After graduating from San Francisco State University in International Business, I dove headfirst into the startup world at Mango Leopard — managing a 22,000-unit supply chain portfolio across international freight corridors, coordinating shipments from Bangladesh, racing against deadlines, and learning what it actually means to run operations under pressure.

But the more I worked, the more I realized something: I could see the problems clearly — I just didn't always have the tools to solve them at the depth they deserved. I was making decisions with gut instinct and spreadsheets when the situation was calling for something more rigorous, more precise.

"I wasn't lacking ambition or work ethic. I was missing a language — the language of data, of models, of turning uncertainty into strategy. So I decided to learn it properly."

I applied to Johns Hopkins Carey Business School — and was admitted with a scholarship. Studying Business Analytics & AI has been exactly what I needed: not just learning new tools, but fundamentally changing how I think. Now I approach every problem with both the operational instincts I built in the field and the analytical rigor to back them up.

PROJECT // 01
Python for Data Analysis
Fuel Inventory Intelligence
Cost Optimization & Risk Management Across a Gas Station Network
1 Business Problem

A regional fuel network across 8 stations lacked a data-driven inventory framework. Replenishment cycles ranged 0.7–33 days. 2,500+ transactions missed volume discount thresholds.

2 Data
  • 5 datasets: fuel levels, invoices, tanks, locations
  • 1,859,658 fuel records · 2,831 cleaned invoices
  • Merged via left joins on tank_id & station_location
3 Approach
  • Integrated into unified 1.86M+ row analytical dataset
  • Built tiered discount model revealing missed savings
  • IQR-based outlier filtering on price-per-liter
  • 5-year NPV analysis on tank expansion
4 Key Insights
  • Thursday = optimal purchase day
  • ~$12.3M CAD projected savings
  • Station 1 top NPV candidate (~$124K vs $100K cost)
  • Safety-triggered refill cuts stockout risk

PROJECT // 02
Operations Research · Stochastic Optimization
BaaS Nairobi: Battery-as-a-Service
Two-Stage Stochastic LP with Tiered SLA Penalties
1 Business Problem

A 50-unit Nairobi complex needed to replace diesel backup with a 200 kWh battery. When to charge vs discharge given time-of-use tariffs and outage uncertainty — and who gets served first?

2 Data
  • KPLC tariffs: off-peak $0.15, peak $0.25/kWh
  • 3 outage scenarios from World Bank data
  • 200 kWh Li-ion · BloombergNEF degradation
3 Approach
  • Two-stage stochastic LP + non-anticipativity constraints
  • Tiered SLA: Premium (40%, $10/kWh) vs Basic (60%)
  • Validated cross-platform: Gurobi + Excel Solver
  • Sensitivity on Premium share α: 0%–100%
4 Key Insights
  • Expected daily cost: $107.96 — cross-platform validated
  • $13.86 (15%) = cost of operating without foresight
  • Zero unserved Premium energy across all scenarios
  • Cost kink at 62% Premium = critical business threshold
